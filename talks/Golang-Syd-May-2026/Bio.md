Bruce Smith is a retired software nerd with a 47-year career that started in the high-stakes world of UNIVAC mainframes. Over nearly half a century, he has managed systems for airlines, national governments, and police forces across Australia, New Zealand and Asia, eventually retiring as a global technical lead for monitoring tools.

While he might be one of the few people in the room who remembers life before the microprocessor, Bruce remains firmly planted in the future. These days, he spends his time building with Go and Gleam, specifically using Lustre to rethink web application architecture. When he isn't bridging the gap between TypeScript and Gleam, Bruce is a dedicated citizen scientist and photographer of Australian native orchids. He brings a unique "long-view" perspective to modern functional programming, proving that great code is ageless.
